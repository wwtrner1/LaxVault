Goalie arc warm-up

Goalie · 10 min · 2+ players · Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced

Add to my practice, freeBuild a whole practice

Setup

Coach shoots from five spots around the arc: left GLE, left wing, middle, right wing, right GLE. Goalie steps to each shooter on the arc and saves. Start soft, then game speed.

Coaching points

  • Step to the ball with the top leg, stick leads
  • Stay on the arc: one step off the pipe at GLE, top of the crease in the middle
  • Watch the ball into the stick
  • Clear it: every save becomes an outlet pass
