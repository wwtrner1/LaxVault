Cradle shuttle sprints

Conditioning · 6 min · 4+ players · Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced

Add to my practice, freeBuild a whole practice

Setup

Cones at 5, 10 and 15 yards. Cradle out to 5 and back, 10 and back, 15 and back. Switch hands on every turn. Run in waves so players rest while others go.

Coaching points

  • Two hands on the stick when you turn
  • Switch hands at every cone
  • Ball stays in the stick: drop it, start over
  • Sprint through the finish line

Gear

Balls, Cones
