Mirror drill (defensive footwork)

Defense · 6 min · 2+ players · Beginner, Intermediate

Add to my practice, freeBuild a whole practice

Setup

Pairs face each other between two cones 5 yards apart. The attacker shuffles side to side and changes direction; the defender mirrors and stays in front. 20 seconds, then switch.

Coaching points

  • Stay low on the balls of your feet
  • Shuffle: feet never cross
  • Watch the hips, not the head or stick
  • Keep the stick out in front, top hand up

Gear

Cones
