3v2 fast break

Game play · 12 min · 6+ players · Intermediate, Advanced

Add to my practice, freeBuild a whole practice

Setup

Three offensive players start at midfield, two defenders and a goalie on the crease. Coach passes in; the offense attacks a 3v2 and finds the open man.

Coaching points

  • Push it: make the defense commit
  • Ball carrier draws a defender, then moves it
  • Spread to a triangle: two low, one high
  • Defense: stack, take away the inside, make them throw

Gear

Balls, Goal
