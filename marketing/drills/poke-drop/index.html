Poke and drop

Defense · 6 min · 2+ players · Beginner

Add to my practice, freeBuild a whole practice

Setup

Pairs: one cradles in a small box, the other defends. The defender pokes at the stick's bottom hand or head; if the ball drops, scoop and become the attacker.

Coaching points

  • Short, quick poke: don't lunge
  • Recover your feet after every check
  • Never check the head or neck
  • Scoop through the loose ball and go
