Ground ball race to goal (1v1)

Ground balls · 10 min · 4+ players · Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced

Add to my practice, freeBuild a whole practice

Setup

Two lines side by side at the restraining line. Coach rolls a ball out front. First pair races; whoever scoops it attacks the goal, the other plays defense. Then the next pair.

Coaching points

  • Get low: bend the knees, not the back
  • Stick parallel to the ground, bottom hand low
  • Run through the ball, don't stop at it
  • Scoop, two hands, head up, then go
