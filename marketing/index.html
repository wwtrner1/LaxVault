How it works

Three choices and one button.

Tell LaxVault who you're coaching, what kind of night it is and how long you have the field. You get a timed script with a drill, setup and coaching points for every period, ready to print or run live.

  1. Who are you coaching?

    U8 to U10, U11 to U12, middle school or high school. The plan picks age-right drills and the right number of touches.

  2. What kind of night?

    A normal practice, game tomorrow, or a first practice with new players. Each one changes the shape of the script.

  3. How long do you have the field?

    45 to 120 minutes. Blocks stretch or drop so the script lands on your time, and the closing game takes what's left.

Live practice mode

A clock the whole team can read from across the field.

Prop your phone on the bench. The period clock runs big, the drill and its coaching points sit under it, and the next period is on deck. A horn sounds at zero and the clock keeps counting in red if you run over.

  • Screen stays awake while it runs
  • Prev, −1, +1, Next from the sideline
  • Sleep or reload keeps the right time
Designer

Drop a 2-3-1, draw the cuts, watch it run.

Men's field, women's field and box floor, half and full, drawn to spec. Tap a set and the whole offense lands around the near goal: 2-3-1 or 1-4-1 on the men's field, 1-4-2 or 2-3-2 on the women's, 2-2-1 or the power play on box, with man-to-man and zones to match. Draw cut, pass, pick and dodge lines, then press Run play and it moves step by step: players cut, then the ball flies.

  • Run play one step at a time
  • Attach a Hudl or YouTube clip to any play or drill
  • Export to PDF and PowerPoint

Send one link or QR code. Players tap their position and see their job on every play, running step by step, with no account and nothing to install. The play quiz asks "what does the A on the left do?" and the scores come back to you, so you know who learned the plays before practice.
